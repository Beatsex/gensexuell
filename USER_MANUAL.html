User manual · beta 0.2.0 · beatsexuell

gensexuell

Grow sounds, don't generate songs.

  • VST3 · CLAP · LV2 · AU · standalone
  • 32 voices
  • runs fully local
  • Windows · macOS (Intel + Apple)
01What it is

A synthesizer you grow.

Drag branches out of a seed. Each branch is the same sound, a little further away. Keep the one you like and grow on from there.

  • A seed is a complete sound: 35 genes for envelopes, oscillators, filter and effect.
  • Twelve branches grow around it, one per key of the octave. The longer a branch, the further its sound has wandered from the seed.
  • Sow a branch and it becomes the new seed, with twelve fresh branches around it. Repeat until the sound is yours.
  • Three more ways to a seed: describe it in words, bring a sample and let it be rebuilt, or play the seed with your own performance.
  • Everything runs on your computer: no cloud, no account, no internet connection. gensexuell makes sounds, never finished music.
02Getting started

Five steps to the first sound.

In a DAW

  1. Insert gensexuell as an instrument on a MIDI track: VST3 / CLAP / AU › beatsexuell › gensexuell.
  2. Play a few keys. Each key of the octave plays its own branch; the branch you play lights up.
  3. Drag a branch out of the seed with the mouse. The further out, the stranger it gets. Its length shows above the seed.
  4. Like what you hear? Click SOW (or the seed in the middle). The branch becomes the new seed.
  5. Save it with Ctrl+S, or just save your project: the whole sound is stored with it.

Standalone

Start gensexuell from the Start menu (or Applications). Choose your audio and MIDI device under Options. No MIDI keyboard? Click the keys along the bottom, or play the branches on your computer keyboard: A W S E D F T G Y H U J.

04Growing

From a seed to your sound.

  • A seed 35 genes, one complete sound
  • Twelve branches each one wanders off in its own direction
  • Drag a branch out longer = further from the seed
  • Listen play its key, or the branch on the ring
  • Sow the branch becomes the new seed
  • Repeat until it is yours, then save
⤢Drag a branch

Grab its square and pull. It also plays while you drag.

SOW

The selected branch becomes the new seed, with twelve new branches. Also: click the seed in the middle.

✱NEW SEED

Start over from a random seed.

⧉CLONE

All twelve branches take the direction and length of the selected one, so every key plays it.

↻REROLL

The selected branch grows in a new random direction.

↶Undo · Redo

Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z. Every sow, drag and slider move can be undone.

Atonality decides how wild branches are: low keeps them in tune and close to the seed, high lets them drift into metallic and noisy places.
05Playing

Which branch a key plays.

Pick it under BULB. Try it here.

01Standard

Each note of the octave plays its own branch: C plays the C branch, in every octave.

02Velocity

How hard you hit picks the branch: the bottom branch for the hardest hits. One sound that changes with your touch.

03Ranges

Each branch covers half an octave, from C1 upward: a split across the keyboard.

04Layered

Every key plays all branches you switched on at once. Click a key on the ring to switch its branch on or off.

And the rest

  • Rotation: drag the small knob on top of the ring to turn which branch sits on which key.
  • Voice: Poly (32 voices), Mono, or Legato (notes slide without a new attack). Glide sets the slide time.
  • Mod wheel: the outer ring shows it; drag it with the mouse too. WHEEL picks what it does: grow all branches longer or shorter, open or close the filter, longer or shorter envelopes, more FM, more LFO, more effect, or volume. Aftertouch moves it as well (MIDI settings).
  • Sync: locks the LFO and looping envelopes to the song tempo, from 1/2 to 1/8.
06Describe a sound

Type it, in German or English.

gensexuell reads your words as qualities of the sound and searches for seeds that really sound that way.

  1. Click DESCRIBE in the left column and type, for example dark soft bass, glassy pluck, not too bright or wärmeres Pad mit viel Raum.
  2. Press Enter or GROW. After a moment, four takes appear, the best match first.
  3. Click a take to load it. It is a normal seed: drag branches, sow, save.
  4. GROW again: four new takes for the same words.

What it understands

  • Kinds of sound: pad, bass, pluck, lead, bell, kick, snare, hat, drone, fx, texture … (Fläche, Zupf, Glocke …). Bass words also tune the seed an octave down.
  • Qualities: warm, bright, dark, glassy, dirty, soft, hard, short, long, metallic, hollow, airy, wobbly, detuned … and their German forms, inflected too (dunkleres, wärmeren).
  • very / sehr pushes a word, slightly / etwas softens it, not / nicht / kein turns it around.
  • Small typos are forgiven. Below the takes you see which words were understood; words it does not know are shown in grey and ignored.
The words describe the sound, not knobs: “bright” means a bright result, however the genes get there.
07Sound → seed

Bring a one-shot, get a seed.

A neural net makes the first guess, four strands keep refining it while you listen.

  1. Drop a one-shot (WAV, FLAC, MP3, OGG) on Sound → seed, or click it and LOAD SAMPLE.
  2. Mark the part to use on the waveform: drag the two edges. PLAY hears it.
  3. Press START. Four strands search side by side; each dot is a better match, the number its similarity.
  4. Click any dot to hear it (AUTO PREVIEW plays each new best one). The one you like: it becomes the seed.
  5. PAUSE when it is close enough, START to go on, RESET to start again.
  • Tune to key off: C4 plays the sample's own pitch. On: the seed is tuned so the detected note plays where it belongs on the keyboard.
  • INFO shows the detected pitch and what the search is doing.
  • Short, dry, single notes work best. Chords, loops and long reverbs are hard to rebuild.
08Performance → seed

Your timing, gensexuell's sound.

Drop a recording of one voice: humming, singing, a bass line, a lead. gensexuell plays it back with the current seed.

  1. Drop a monophonic recording on Perform, or click it and LOAD RECORDING.
  2. The detected notes appear as bars over the waveform. Adjust if needed (below).
  3. RENDER: the notes play through the current seed with all its effects.
  4. PLAY ORIGINAL and PLAY RESULT to compare.
  5. Drag the WAV or MIDI tile straight onto a track in your DAW, from the dialog or from the left column. Click a tile to see the file.
≈Smooth

Higher: steadier pitch, fewer short wobbles taken as notes.

⌐Min note

Notes shorter than this (20 … 300 ms) are dropped.

∿Bends

On: vibrato and slides are kept as pitch bend. Off: clean notes only.

±Octave

Plays the result up to two octaves higher or lower.

Renders are saved as WAV and MIDI in Documents\Seedling\Renders, with the original length plus three seconds of tail.

09Live input

Play it with your voice, in real time.

  1. Route audio into gensexuell's sidechain input in your DAW (Standalone: the audio input under Options).
  2. Switch LIVE INPUT on. The meter shows the level, the big note what gensexuell hears.
  3. Sing or play one note at a time. gensexuell follows with the current seed.
dBGate

Quieter than this is silence. Raise it if breaths or room noise trigger notes.

⌒Glide

How smoothly the pitch follows you between notes.

±Octave

Two octaves up or down.

∿Own envelope · Follow level

The seed's own envelope, or the loudness of your input shapes the sound.

Live input plays one voice at a time and reacts within about 20–60 ms: tight enough to play, not sample-accurate. For exact timing use Performance → Seed.
10Genes & FX

Under the hood, if you want it.

Genes

ALL 35 GENES opens the seed's 35 genes in three tabs: Envelope & LFO, Oscillators, Filter & Effect, each with a small graph of what it does. Changing a gene changes the seed, and every branch with it. Ctrl+click a slider to reset it.

1Envelope & LFO

Attack, decay, sustain, loop, the mod envelope, vibrato or tremolo.

2Oscillators

Two waves, their ratio, FM, noise, a sub, a pitch sweep.

3Filter

Cutoff, resonance, type, a second stage, key tracking, drive.

4Effect

Chorus, Haze reverb, Dusk low-pass, bass and treble, stereo spread.

Grain FX

Four grain engines from grainsexuell sit after the voices, on the whole sound. Pick one and a variant, then shape it with Swarm, Tail, Span, Scatter and Mix. Haze and Dusk sit right beside them in the main window.

EngineVariantsWhat it does
MosaicStack · Low · High · TumbleRearranges small pieces of the sound into a moving texture.
CloudMist · Shimmer · Murk · BackwashA cloud of long grains: pads, haze, octaves, backwards washes.
ShardShuffle · Crush · Arp · StutterChops and rearranges in rhythm: glitches, stutters, arpeggios.
TapEcho · Ping-pong · Reverse · CascadeGrain delays that follow the song tempo.
11Patches & MIDI

Keep it, recall it, control it.

Patches

  • Click the patch name for the list: 12 factory sounds, the other patches in the same folder, OPEN… and SAVE…. Ctrl+click the name: a random factory sound.
  • ‹ › step to the previous or next patch in the folder.
  • Patches are small .seedling files (the name from before gensexuell had its name, kept so old patches still open). COPY PATCH and PASTE PATCH (☰) move one through the clipboard.
  • Your DAW project always stores the whole sound, saved or not.

Helpers in ☰

  • Correct tuning: measures the seed and tunes it to C.
  • Normalize volume and Normalize branch volumes: even levels for the seed and for all twelve branches. BRANCH VOLUMES… sets each one by hand.

MIDI

  • MIDI controllers…: click LEARN next to a control and move a knob on your controller.
  • MIDI settings…: input channel, velocity curve, aftertouch → wheel, polyphony, pitch wheel range (up to 48 semitones, MPE-friendly), master tuning, whether MIDI notes select branches, program change. MAKE DEFAULT keeps them for new instances.
  • Program list…: store up to 16 patches and recall them with MIDI program change.
  • Every main control, mode, FX setting, branch length and gene is a parameter your DAW can automate.
12Tips & troubleshooting

Ways to grow, and what to do when it doesn't.

  • Small steps: short branches, sow often. Long branches jump far and are hard to come back from.
  • Lost? Undo is unlimited within a session.
  • Words first, then grow: describe roughly, pick the best take, then refine it by growing.
  • A pad on every key: CLONE, then lower Atonality.
  • Humming to bass line: Performance → Seed with a bass seed, octave −1, then drag the MIDI into your DAW.
The search in Sound → Seed stays low.
The sample has more going on than one voice can make: chords, a loop, a long reverb or room. Mark a shorter, drier part. Some sounds can only be approached, never copied.
Performance → Seed finds wrong notes.
It expects one voice at a time. Raise Smooth and Min note, or use a drier recording. Breaths and consonants are skipped on purpose.
Live input plays nothing.
Check that audio reaches the sidechain (the meter moves) and that LIVE INPUT is on. If the meter moves but no note appears, lower Gate. “NO SIDECHAIN” means the DAW sends nothing to it.
The sound is quiet and dull.
Look at the bottom right of the seed: with the mod wheel on FILTER − the filter starts closed and only opens as the wheel comes up. Turn the wheel up, or pick another target under WHEEL. Each sound keeps its own level: Normalize volume in ☰ evens it out.
Dragging WAV or MIDI into the DAW does nothing.
Render first. Some DAWs only accept drops onto an empty track area; click the tile to open the folder and drag from there.
Text → Seed ignores my word.
Grey words are unknown. Try a simpler or English synonym; “glasig” and “glassy” mean the same.
The plugin does not show up.
Rescan plugins in your DAW. Windows: VST3 in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3, CLAP in …\Common Files\CLAP. macOS: AU in ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components.
