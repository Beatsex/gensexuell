gensexuell is installed

  1. Restart your DAW or rescan plugins. gensexuell shows up as an instrument under beatsexuell → gensexuell.
  2. Or open gensexuell from Applications.
  3. Play a few notes, drag a branch out of the seed, make something good the new seed with SOW.

Manual, licenses, source and Uninstall gensexuell are in /Library/Application Support/beatsexuell/gensexuell (Finder: Go → Go to Folder…). Your patches and renders go to Documents/Seedling.
