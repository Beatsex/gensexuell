gensexuell @VERSION@ beta

A synthesizer you grow from a seed, by beatsexuell. Grow sounds, don't generate songs. Everything runs on your Mac: no cloud, no account, no internet connection.

This installs the standalone app and the plugins (VST3, AU, CLAP, LV2) for every user of this Mac. Under Customize you can leave out formats you don't need.

  • Universal: runs natively on Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 10.15 or newer.
  • The beta is not signed by Apple yet. If macOS refused to open this installer, you already found the way: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
