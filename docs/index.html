Under the hood

Words describe the sound,
never the knobs.

Every candidate seed is rendered for 1.5 seconds and measured on nine descriptors, all scaled 0–1. Your words set targets and weights; the search keeps whatever lands closest.

Lexicon

Every word it knows.

The complete vocabulary. Stems match word endings, so “warm” also catches “wärmeres”. Umlauts are written as ae, oe, ue.

Three ways to a sound

Describe. Grow. Bring a sample.

01

Describe it

Type what you want. gensexuell draws 240 seeds, measures each, refines the best four with a short evolution and ranks them by fit.

02

Grow it

Twelve branches around a seed; the longer the branch, the further from the original. Sow, clone, reroll, undo.

03

Bring a sample

Drop a one-shot. A neural net guesses the genes, then four strands refine the match while you listen.
